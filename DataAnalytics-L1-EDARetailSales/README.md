
EDA on Retail Sales Dataset - Task 1 Level 1

For this project, I analyzed a retail sales transaction dataset to uncover patterns in customer behavior, sales trends, and product performance. Here's what I did:
🔹 Cleaned and explored the dataset (1,000 transactions, zero missing values/duplicates)
 🔹 Computed descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, std dev)
 🔹 Analyzed time-series trends to identify peak sales months
 🔹 Studied customer demographics (age & gender distribution)
 🔹 Identified top-performing product categories
 🔹 Built a correlation heatmap to understand relationships between variables
 🔹 Derived actionable business recommendations from the findings
